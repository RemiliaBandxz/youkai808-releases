Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Payments

Payments are processed by Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your name, email, billing details and payment information to complete your order, under Paddle's Privacy Policy. We never see or store your full card details.

What we receive

From your order we receive your email address, the product you bought, and your country, so we can deliver your license key and support you.

License activation

When you activate a Product, the Hub sends your license key and an anonymous computer ID (a hash, not your name or files) to our license server so the key works on your machines and can't be shared. The Hub also checks for updates. No audio, project files, or personal files ever leave your computer.

This website

This site has no ads, no tracking pixels and no analytics cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security.

Your rights

You can ask us to show or delete the data we hold about you. For order and payment data, you can also contact Paddle directly at paddle.net.

Changes

We may update this policy. The version on this page is the one that applies.
